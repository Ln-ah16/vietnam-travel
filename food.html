Popular Vietnamese Dishes

Vietnamese cuisine uses fresh herbs, vegetables, noodles, rice, and a variety of flavorful ingredients.

Pho

Pho is a famous Vietnamese noodle soup served with broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat.

Spring Rolls

Vietnamese spring rolls are often made with fresh vegetables, herbs, noodles, and meat or shrimp.

Banh Mi

Banh mi is a Vietnamese sandwich combining crispy bread with savory fillings and fresh vegetables.

Food and Culture

Food is an important part of Vietnamese culture. Different regions have their own ingredients, cooking styles, and traditional dishes.
